Terms of Service

General conditions governing transport, haulage, and commercial agreements.

1. General Framework & Scope

These General Conditions govern all service contracts, online bookings, physical waybills, and commercial agreements between the client (individual passenger, commercial consignor, or corporate entity) and ASIL Logistics & Transportation Services Co. Ltd. By submitting a travel reservation or cargo consignment request, the customer acknowledges and agrees to these terms in full.

2. Passenger Transportation Provisions

  • Booking Review: All passenger travel requests submitted via the portal remain in Pending status until verified by our fleet dispatch control team. Confirmation is provided upon operational validation and vehicle scheduling.
  • Luggage Allowances: Standard individual bookings permit reasonable personal luggage per passenger. Excess luggage or oversized baggage must be declared at the time of reservation.
  • Check-in & Boarding: Passengers must arrive at designated departure terminals at least 30 minutes before the scheduled linehaul departure time. Valid government-issued identification is required.

3. Cargo & Freight Distribution Provisions

  • Consignment Declaration: The consignor must provide complete, accurate descriptions of cargo contents, estimated gross weight, package dimensions, and special handling instructions.
  • Prohibited & Hazardous Articles: ASIL Logistics does not carry prohibited contraband, unregistered flammables, dangerous hazardous chemicals without prior certified manifest, or illegal narcotics. ASIL Logistics reserves the right to inspect cargo packages for road safety compliance.
  • Packaging Integrity: The consignor is responsible for ensuring goods are properly packed and cushioned for domestic and regional transit. ASIL Logistics provides vehicle lashing and secure tie-downs during transit.
  • Delivery Milestones: Delivery confirmations must be executed by the designated receiver upon receipt of goods and inspection of exterior seals.

4. Corporate Accounts & Onboarding

Corporate entities submitting onboarding requests agree to furnish authentic commercial registry documentation and tax identification certificates. Corporate contract rates, scheduled linehaul allowances, and invoicing terms become effective upon mutual execution of the corporate transport agreement.

5. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

ASIL Logistics operates under rigorous road safety, vehicle preventative maintenance, and route planning standards. ASIL Logistics shall not be held liable for transit delays caused by extreme weather occurrences, civil road blockades, official highway closures, or acts of force majeure beyond reasonable operational control.

For commercial inquiries, specialized project cargo rates, or corporate charter agreements, contact our central operations management team via the contact desk.
